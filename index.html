The Legacy

Ink that outlasts the trends.

Dody opened his doors in Gonzales in 1990, back when a tattoo was a handshake and a steady hand. More than three decades later, Tattoos By Dody is still standing on Airline Highway — one of the oldest, busiest studios in south Louisiana, and one of the most trusted names for miles around Ascension Parish.

Generations of clients have walked through that door: first tattoos, memorials, cover-ups of somebody else's mistake, and pieces they'd been planning for years. The shop's built its reputation the old-fashioned way — clean work, fair prices, and artists who actually care what ends up on your skin.

Take Care of It

Aftercare, quick version

  1. Leave the wrap on as long as your artist tells you — usually a few hours.
  2. Wash gently with clean hands and mild, unscented soap. Pat dry.
  3. Thin layer of ointment or fragrance-free lotion, a couple times a day.
  4. No soaking, no sun. Skip pools, baths and tanning while it heals.
  5. Don't pick or scratch. Let it flake and settle on its own.

Questions while it heals? Call the shop — we'd rather you ask than guess.
